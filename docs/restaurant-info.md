## How dine-in, pickup and delivery work
- Customers choose the restaurant, then Dine in (with the number on their table), Pickup or Delivery. A table QR code carries the restaurant and the table and skips both questions.
- The restaurant admin sees every order tagged Dine-in · Table N, Pickup or Delivery, confirms it, marks it ready, then served or completed. There is no separate waiter app.
- Customers get 2 minutes to cancel an order they placed.
