## How ordering works
- Customers choose the restaurant, then dine in (with the number on their table), pickup or delivery. A table QR code carries the restaurant and the table and skips both questions.
- The restaurant admin sees each order marked Dine-in with the table number, Pickup or Delivery, confirms it, marks it ready, then served or completed.
- Customers can cancel for a short time after ordering. After that the kitchen can confirm.

## Logins
