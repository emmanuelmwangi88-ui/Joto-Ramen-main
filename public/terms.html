Terms of Service

Last updated: October 2026

Orders

Orders are placed with the branch you choose. Prices are in Kenya Shillings. Delivery orders have a minimum order value and a delivery fee shown at checkout. Some dishes are not available at every branch, for example dishes containing pork are not served at our Kenrail Towers branch.

Cancelling

You can cancel an order yourself for 2 minutes after you place it. After that the kitchen starts preparing it, so please call the branch if you need a change.

Payment

You can pay with M-Pesa on this website once the cancellation window has ended, or pay at the till or on delivery. Payments are recorded against your order.

Reservations

A reservation request is only confirmed once we reply. Please let us know if you need to cancel or change it.

Reviews

Reviews are checked before they appear. We may remove reviews that are abusive or not about our food and service.

Allergies

Our kitchens handle many ingredients. Please tell us about any allergies in the order notes or to the restaurant.
