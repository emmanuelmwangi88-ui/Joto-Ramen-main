Dashboard

Every M-Pesa request and every cash or manual payment is saved here. Pending means the customer has not entered their PIN yet.

Payment accounts

Every restaurant currently uses the shared demo account: customers can try the M-Pesa prompt but no real money moves. When a restaurant's own paybill or till has been approved by Safaricom (Go Live), enter its number and Daraja details below, save, then switch Live payments on for that restaurant. Until then the switch stays locked. The keys are stored where only the server can read them and are never shown again.

Change a price by typing in the price box and pressing Enter. Use the switch to show or hide a dish.

Published events appear on your website under "What's On". Past events are hidden automatically.
Only active locations show on the website and in the reservation form.

Table QR codes

Print these and stick one on each table. Guests scan, see the menu, and order to that table. They can also call a waiter or ask for the bill.

Restaurant admins

Each restaurant has its own admin login. A restaurant admin sees and manages ONLY their own restaurant: its orders, reviews, reservations, payments, menu availability and events. They cannot see the other restaurants. Give them the email and password; they can change the password in Settings.

M-Pesa payments

When on, customers can pay for an order from the website once the 2-minute cancel window ends. Turn it on only after the Daraja secrets are added in Supabase (see the README, "M-Pesa").

Order alerts

New orders play a loud chime, vibrate your phone and show a notification. Keep this page open (or installed on your phone) on the device by the kitchen. Browsers need one tap on the page before they allow sound.
