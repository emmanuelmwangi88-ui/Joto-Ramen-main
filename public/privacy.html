Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Joto Ramen ("we") runs restaurants in Nairobi, Kenya, and this website. This page explains what information we collect and why.

What we collect

When you order, book a table, review a dish or create an account we collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery address (for deliveries), your order and any notes you write. If you pay with M-Pesa we receive the transaction receipt number and the phone number used; we never see your M-Pesa PIN.

Why we use it

To prepare and deliver your order, confirm bookings, take payment, contact you about your order, and improve our menu and service. We only send marketing emails if you subscribe to our newsletter, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Who sees it

Our staff at the branch that serves you, and our payment provider (Safaricom M-Pesa) for payments. Our website is hosted on Vercel and our data is stored with Supabase. We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep order and payment records for as long as the law requires for accounting, and other information until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information. Contact us on the number or Instagram account shown on the website.
